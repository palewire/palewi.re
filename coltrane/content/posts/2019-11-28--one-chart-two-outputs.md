<p>Today's Los Angeles Times features the first web charts shipped to print with no extra production.</p>

<p>No Adobe Illustrator. No CMYK conversion. No resizing.</p>

<p>One chart. Two outputs.</p>

<p>It's all thanks to Datawrapper tech, linked up with our system by Thomas Suh Lauder and Lorena Elebee.</p>

<p>It's fitting these charts appear in the Thanksgiving edition. They represent a significant streamlining of print work in the Data and Graphics Department.</p>

<p>Until today it required hours of staff time to double-produce simple charts for web and print.</p>

<p>Now, thanks to Datawrapper, it's this easy.</p>
