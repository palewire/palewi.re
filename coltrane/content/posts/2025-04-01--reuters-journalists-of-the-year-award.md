<p>A special delivery: the Reuters Journalists of the Year Award!</p>

<p>It recognizes our team's work integrating data into the daily news report. The drive is still in its early days, but loyal readers should increasingly see live, interactive graphics appear above the fold.</p>

<p>An example came just last night, when a real-time market chart anchored the top cluster in the shiny new Reuters iPhone app.</p>

<p>You can <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reuters-news/id602660809">download the Reuters iPhone app</a> today.</p>
