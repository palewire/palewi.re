<p>I finally found it. The first computer-assisted data journalism to run in the Los Angeles Times.</p>

<p>The Sunday July 29, 1984 paper featured Richard O'Reilly's smog chart of air conditions for the Olympic games, which opened in LA the day before.</p>

<p>Here it is.</p>

<img src="/static/img/first-computer-assisted-data-journalism-2.jpg" alt="The Los Angeles Times' Saturday's Smog Levels charts for July 28, 1984.">

<p>Dick says:</p>

<blockquote>
<p>I downloaded the data from the AQMD's dial-up data feed and fed the numbers into a BASIC program which fed the plot values into an HP Pen plotter.</p>

<p>The plots were printed onto acetate, which was overlaid in the composing room onto page proofs that already had the labels and grid lines that were constants.</p>

<p>After the Olympics, the editors liked the smog feature enough to make it a permanent feature, putting it at the bottom of the weather page, giving up an advertising strip across the bottom to do so.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Dick says he analyzed the data on a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compaq_Portable">Compaq "Portable" personal computer</a>, which came with a handle and weighed nearly 30 pounds.</p>

<img src="/static/img/first-computer-assisted-data-journalism-3.jpg" alt="A Compaq Portable personal computer with its keyboard detached.">

<img src="/static/img/first-computer-assisted-data-journalism-4.jpg" alt="The back of a Compaq Portable personal computer, showing its handle and connectors.">

<p>Dick says his son, then a high school student, helped write that first BASIC program to analyze smog data. He later learned SAS, which he used for the rest of his programming career.</p>

<p>The success of the Olympics project led to the creation of the Los Angeles Times' first computer-assisted reporting team.</p>

<p>Dick was happy that allowed him to hire "a real programmer." His first partner was Maurine Lyons. When she left, he was joined by Sandra Poindexter from RAND.</p>

<p>Sandy continued to work at the Los Angeles Times until last year, when she retired.</p>
