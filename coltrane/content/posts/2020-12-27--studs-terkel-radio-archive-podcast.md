<p>My favorite pandemic pastime has been tuning in to <a href="https://studsterkel.wfmt.com/">WFMT's Studs Terkel Radio Archive</a>.</p>

<p>This weekend, I repackaged its collection as a podcast feed. It will deliver an interview from the legendary Chicago broadcaster several times a week.</p>

<p>Subscribe now at <a href="https://studs.show/">studs.show</a>.</p>

<p>Like my other archival experiments, all of the code that powers the site is <a href="https://github.com/pastpages/studs-terkel-podcast">open source on GitHub</a>.</p>

<p>The repository takes advantage of GitHub's powerful new <a href="https://github.com/pastpages/studs-terkel-podcast/actions?query=workflow%3ABuild">Actions framework</a> to automatically republish the RSS feed, and ship out the latest podcasts, at midnight each night.</p>

<p>Episodes will be released on the anniversary of their broadcast. The feed is preloaded with December interviews from years past.</p>

<p>The first: <a href="https://studsterkel.wfmt.com/programs/interviewing-shel-silverstein">Shel Silverstein's Dec. 12, 1963 visit</a>. He hates on Chicago so hard, Studs' legendary empathy is put to the test.</p>

<p>Subscribe today at <a href="https://studs.show/">studs.show</a> to receive January interviews with Saul Alinsky, Harry Belafonte, Gwendolyn Brooks and more. That's <a href="https://studs.show/">studs.show</a>.</p>
