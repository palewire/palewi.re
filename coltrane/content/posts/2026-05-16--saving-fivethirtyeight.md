<p>ABC News killed FiveThirtyEight's website, pulling down thousands of data journalism stories and dashboards.</p>

<p>I can't bring the pages back, but I can offer you <a href="https://fivethirtyeightindex.com/">fivethirtyeightindex</a>, a new website listing everything captured by the Internet Archive. I hope it's helpful to someone out there.</p>
